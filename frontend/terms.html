What this tool is

Inspeckt is an automated first-pass compliance assessment tool, not a law firm and not FSSAI itself. Reports and Formulation Lab scores reflect the regulatory data encoded in this build at the time you use it — they are not a legal opinion, an FSSAI approval, or a guarantee your product is compliant. Always verify against the current gazetted FSSAI regulations before production, labelling, or sale.

Accounts

Sign-in is email-based with no password. In this build, submitting the sign-up or log-in form signs you in immediately — a real deployment would send a one-time link to your email first.
