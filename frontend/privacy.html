Privacy

Privacy policy

This is a plain-language summary, not a lawyer-drafted legal document — treat it as informational, and check with your own counsel before relying on it for a business decision.

What we collect

Account sign-up asks for your name, business name, FSSAI license number, and email. New Submission and Formulation Lab ask for formulation and lab-test figures you choose to enter. The "Talk to a consultant" form asks for your name, email, phone (optional), and message.

Where it's stored

In this build, everything above is stored only in your own browser's local storage — it is not sent to or stored on any Inspeckt server. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. Consult-form submissions are additionally offered as a pre-filled email you send yourself from your own email client — that message goes to Inspeckt's inbox, and is subject to your own email provider's handling.

Your choices

You can clear everything this tool has stored at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by asking us to delete anything you've emailed us.
