What gets checked

Every submission is checked against four testing parameters

Live today for Dairy; the same structure extends to every FSSAI category as its data is compiled and signed off.

%

Composition

Fat, SNF, moisture and fat-on-dry-basis against the exact minimums/maximums for your declared product type.

Testing Parameters

Process-hygiene limits (like plate count, coliform) and food-safety limits (like Salmonella, Listeria) — with the 2/3-class acceptance logic FSSAI actually uses.

Labelling & claims

Mandatory elements — FSSAI license, allergen box, batch number, dates — plus claims, checked once a real lab report backs them.

Shelf life

A storage-temperature and shelf-life recommendation based on your heat treatment or cheese type.

Why Inspeckt

Built by a food-safety team, not just a form builder

Every number is cited

Each check links back to its exact FSSAI clause — e.g. "FSS 2.1.2, Ch 1.2 — Milk Type 1 (Buffalo Milk)" — so you can verify it yourself or hand it to an auditor.

Three-state results

Not just PASS/FAIL — a WARNING state flags borderline microbiology results that sit between the acceptable and rejection limits, so you catch process drift early.

We never guess a number

Nutrition panel values and claims are only ever populated from a real, uploaded NABL lab report — never estimated from what you typed in.

Nothing leaves your browser

This version stores your submissions locally in your browser. No formulation data touches a server unless you choose to run the optional backend.
